Full-Service HVAC Care for Brooklyn Homes and Businesses

We service every type of heating and cooling equipment you'll find in local homes and businesses, from central air conditioners and gas furnaces to heat pumps and ductless mini-splits. It doesn't matter whether the building is a starter home, a townhome, or a storefront. Our technicians understand the local weather, the age and construction of local homes, and the code requirements inspectors enforce, which pays off in every diagnosis.

Most homeowners we work with need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or regular maintenance. Repairs tend to be straightforward: a failed capacitor, a corroded flame sensor, a refrigerant leak, or a worn-out blower motor. Replacements get a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling its way to an early failure. Tune-ups cover coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, and nothing does more to extend equipment life.

For commercial property owners and managers nearby, we service the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air and ventilation equipment that restaurants, shops, offices, and apartment buildings depend on. Fast HVAC Response in Brooklyn

When your system quits, how fast help shows up matters more than anything else. That's why we keep our schedule flexible across the area and answer every call with a live person. Emergency calls typically see a technician within a day, and we treat no-heat and no-cool calls as top priority.

The emergency calls we answer tend to follow a pattern. On the cooling side, it's an AC blowing warm air, an outdoor unit that hums without starting, or an evaporator coil frozen solid. On the heating side, it's a furnace that lights and then shuts down. Year-round, it's breakers that trip every cycle, clogged condensate drains backing water into the furnace cabinet, and thermostats that stop responding. Because our trucks carry capacitors, contactors, igniters, flame sensors, universal control boards, and fittings, most of these are wrapped up in one visit, at a price you approve before we start.

A little caution while you wait for us can head off a much bigger repair. An iced-over AC should be turned off at the thermostat with the fan set to "on", which lets the coil thaw faster. Running it frozen can flood the drain pan and damage the compressor. A furnace that keeps short-cycling, or any burning smell, means it's time to shut the unit off at its service switch and call us immediately.

How Brooklyn Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of the year.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a miserable night.
