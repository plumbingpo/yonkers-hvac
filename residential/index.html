No Surprises

Straight Answers and Upfront Prices

Before we touch a tool, we figure out exactly what's wrong. Our technicians check the age and tonnage of your system, the refrigerant charge, the heat exchanger and coils, and the condition of your ductwork. Then you get a straightforward recommendation and the full price up front, whether the job is swapping a worn capacitor or replacing the whole system. No hidden fees, no last-minute add-ons.

Most HVAC failures send small signals well before the system stops. Watch for energy bills that creep up, airflow that feels weaker than it used to, frost on the copper lines, or a bedroom that never catches up to the thermostat. Behind those symptoms are usually pinhole refrigerant leaks, worn blower bearings, or a capacitor on its way out. A quick inspection at the first sign often costs far less than a failed compressor.

Do you know how old your system is? When a furnace or AC gets past the 12-year mark, it's worth thinking about the next one before the current one decides for you. Emergency replacements happen on the worst days, in heat waves and cold snaps, when choices are limited and everyone is uncomfortable. Our technicians know how the Yonkers climate wears on equipment and will tell you plainly whether yours has years left or is running on borrowed time.

Maintenance Plans

Seasonal HVAC Maintenance Plans

Each maintenance visit from Yonkers HVAC is far more than a quick filter swap. Our technician cleans the coils, verifies the refrigerant charge, tests capacitors and contactors, inspects the burners and heat exchanger, clears the condensate drain, replaces the filter, and measures static pressure across the ducts. Minor faults get caught while they're still cheap to fix, and your system stays running at full efficiency.

No two maintenance plans look exactly alike. An older furnace gets a closer look than a brand-new heat pump, and a dusty area means condenser coils foul faster. We build spring cooling and fall heating visits around your equipment and climate, so every tune-up keeps the system efficient and extends the life of the equipment you've already paid for.

Think of maintenance as protection for equipment you've already bought. Each tune-up keeps the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, and that lowers monthly energy bills while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most households, a plan costs far less than replacing the system ahead of schedule.

Sized for Your Home

Equipment Sized for Your Actual House

Getting the size right is one of the most overlooked parts of any install. Before recommending new equipment, Yonkers HVAC measures the details that drive your home's heating and cooling load: square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout. The result is a system that runs in long, efficient cycles, controls humidity, and holds every room at the temperature you set.

Homes change over time. An addition or a finished basement can overwhelm the single-stage system that used to be plenty. Modern two-stage and variable-speed heat pumps hold tighter temperatures and use much less energy. Whether you're comparing a heat pump and a furnace-and-AC pair, or ducted and ductless, we'll explain the real trade-offs in SEER2 ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting needs, and installation work.

Repairs We Handle

Common HVAC Problems We Fix in Yonkers

No cooling, a furnace that refuses to light, ice on the indoor coil, a slow refrigerant leak: every one of these problems has its own cause and calls for a different repair. Yonkers HVAC diagnoses the exact fault before replacing anything, whether it's a condenser that hums but won't start, a furnace that short-cycles, a thermostat that keeps losing its program, a clogged condensate drain, or a mini-split flashing error codes.

Some failures can wait a day, and some can't. A weak capacitor only keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can soak through a ceiling before anyone notices. Our technicians carry the parts to swap capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, repair leaky ducts that dump conditioned air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak has been repaired.
